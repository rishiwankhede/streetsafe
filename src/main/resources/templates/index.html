How StreetSafe works

Vendors register

Add your stall with its 14-digit FSSAI number. If the format is valid (14 digits starting with 1 or 2), the badge is awarded automatically.

Stalls display a QR

Each stall gets its own QR code standee that points to its public verification page. Print it and stick it on the cart.

Customers rate it

Customers scan, see the verification status and the hygiene rating, and leave their own 1–5 star review in seconds.
